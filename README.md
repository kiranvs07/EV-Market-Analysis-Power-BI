# EV-Market-Analysis-Power-BI

## Project Overview

This project analyzes electric vehicle market performance using Power BI.

The reports focuses on sales performance, brand and model analysis,
country-level performance, and EV technology and Price.

## Business Objectives

- Analyze overall EV market sales and unit performance
- Identify top-performing brands and models
- Compare EV performance across countries
- Analyze battery capacity, driving range and charging capacity
- Monitor YoY sales and unit growth
- Analyze market share and competitive performance
- Compare the Battery Capacity with the Price 

## Report Pages

### 1. Executive Overview
Provides an overall view of EV market performance through KPIs,
sales trends, growth analysis and market-level insights.

### 2. Brand & Model Analysis
Analyzes brand sales, units sold and top-performing EV models.

### 3. Country Performance
Compares EV sales, units sold and market share across countries.

### 4. EV Technology & Price
Analyzes battery capacity, driving range, charging capacity,
charging time, price and powertrain performance.

## Key Insights

- Total sales analyzed: $28.2B
- Total units sold: 673K
- Average vehicle price: $43.4K
- EV sales increased across the analyzed years.
- Tesla, BYD and Hyundai were among the leading brands.
- Australia, Canada and China were among the leading markets.
- Battery capacity, range, price and charging characteristics were analyzed
  alongside sales performance.
